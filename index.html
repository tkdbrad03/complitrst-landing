Common Questions

Answered before you have to ask.

Does this work for my state?

Yes. CompliTrst covers all 50 states. You will receive a compliance roadmap specific to your state and your facility type. Every state has different requirements. That is exactly why this exists.

What facility types does this cover?

Adult day care, group homes, and assisted living facilities. If you are unsure which path is right for you, the Starter plan will help you understand the differences and requirements for each.

I have not opened yet. Is it too early to start?

No. The Starter plan is built specifically for women in the research and planning phase. Understanding your compliance requirements before you spend money on a location or build-out is how you avoid costly mistakes.

What if my staff is not tech-savvy?

CompliTrst is designed to be used by people running care businesses, not developers. If you can send an email, you can use CompliTrst. Onboarding support is included with every plan.

How long does it take to get set up?

Most clients are fully set up and using the system within 3 to 7 days. You will not wait months to see value. Most clients see ROI within the first 30 days through time saved and fines avoided.

Is my data secure?

Yes. CompliTrst uses enterprise-grade security with encrypted storage and regular backups. Your data is protected and handled in accordance with HIPAA and applicable state regulations.
